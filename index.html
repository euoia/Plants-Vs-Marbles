🐜 Ants vs Marbles

For a more comfortable game view, rotate your phone to landscape.

How to play

  • Stop the ants. They climb up from the hills at the bottom. If one reaches the top, you lose.
  • Drop marbles. Pick one from the bar, then click a lane. The higher you drop it, the harder it lands, and heavy marbles hit hardest.
  • Collect crumbs. Click falling crumbs to pay for more marbles.
  • Marbles queue. They pass through each other, but line up behind one that an ant is chewing.

Track pieces

Ramp
Slides a marble into the next lane.
Spring
Bounces it back up for another drop. Click a placed spring to set its strength.
Boost
Speeds it up so it hits harder.
Ring
Pays crumbs every time a marble drops through.

Controls

1 to 9, 0 - = Pick from the bar
Click a lane Drop the marble or piece
G Glove: pick up and move marbles and pieces
E Ant Eater: clears the maze, then rests 90 s
P Pause
Esc or right-click Cancel
