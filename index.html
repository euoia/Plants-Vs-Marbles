🐜 Ants vs Marbles

Defend a 3D tabletop marble maze: choose a marble, then click a lane to drop it from that height. Higher, heavier drops hit harder. Ramps, rings, springs and boosts make marble tracks; the Ant Eater (E) sweeps the maze. Collect crumbs, pause with P, move pieces with the glove (G), or cancel with Esc / right-click. Move the camera with WASD or arrow keys, zoom with the mouse wheel, hold the middle mouse button to pan, or hold Shift while dragging the middle mouse button to rotate. Runs locally with WebGL; no network connection required.
