🐜 Ants vs Marbles

Pick a marble, then click or tap a lane to drop it in from that height (higher = harder landing on ants; heavier marbles hit harder). Marbles roll toward the exit and stop at ants; they pass through each other unless one is being chewed, in which case the ones behind queue up. Track pieces: Ramps slide marbles into the next lane, Rings pay out crumbs every time a marble drops through, Springs bounce them back up toward the top for another drop — click a placed spring to cycle its strength (1–4 bars) and set how high it bounces. Ant Eater (E) sweeps the whole maze clean — then rests 90 s. Collect crumbs by clicking or tapping them. Right-click / Esc cancels, G = glove (moves marbles and track pieces).

For a more comfortable game view, rotate your phone to landscape.
